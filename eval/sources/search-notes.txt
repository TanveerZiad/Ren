Information Retrieval & Search Systems Notes

1. Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF)
TF-IDF is a numerical statistic intended to reflect how important a word is to a document in a collection or corpus.
- Term frequency (TF) measures how frequently a term occurs in a specific document.
- Inverse document frequency (IDF) diminishes the weight of terms that appear very frequently across the entire corpus (such as common stop words) and increases the weight of rare, specific terms.

2. BM25 Ranking Algorithm
Okapi BM25 is a non-linear probabilistic term-matching function that builds upon TF-IDF principles. It incorporates document length normalization and saturation limits on term frequencies to avoid over-weighting repeated words.

3. Dense Vector Embeddings & Cosine Similarity
Modern semantic search maps sentences into high-dimensional vector spaces. Semantic similarity is measured using cosine similarity between query and passage vectors, capturing synonyms and conceptual paraphrases that keyword search alone might miss.

4. Hybrid Retrieval
Hybrid retrieval combines keyword-based lexical matching (such as BM25 or SQLite FTS5) with dense vector embeddings to achieve high precision on exact acronyms (like TF-IDF) and high recall on semantic variations.
